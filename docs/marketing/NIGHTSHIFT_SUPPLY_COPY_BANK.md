## FAQ Section

**Q: Do you restock?**
> No. Every drop is a fixed run. Once a piece sells out, it's done. That's the point.

**Q: How do I know when the next drop is?**
> Join the list. That's the only place we announce drops in advance.

**Q: What are your sizing options?**
> Most pieces run S–XL. Some limited colorways are M–L only. Check the product page for specifics. When in doubt, size up.

**Q: Can I return or exchange?**
> We accept exchanges on unworn, tagged items within 14 days of delivery. No returns on sale or drop items.

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
> Orders process within 2–3 business days. Domestic shipping is 3–7 days. International varies.

**Q: Where are the pieces made?**
> We work with a small roster of production partners. Each drop lists its origin in the product details.

**Q: Is this sustainable?**
> Small-batch production is inherently less wasteful than mass retail. We don't make more than we can sell.

**Q: Do you do wholesale or stockist inquiries?**
> Yes. Reach out via the contact page with your shop details.
