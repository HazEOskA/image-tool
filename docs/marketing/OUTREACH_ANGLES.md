## 5 Objections and Answers

Practical responses to the most common pushback. Don't be defensive — validate and redirect.

---

**Objection 1: "We already have a Shopify site."**

> Totally — Shopify handles the backend well. What I build is the front — the landing experience, the drop page, the motion layer that makes people actually want to stay and buy. I can also integrate with an existing Shopify store so the checkout still runs through it. The two work well together.

---

**Objection 2: "It's not in the budget right now."**

> Makes sense. The Starter tier starts at $600 — it's designed for exactly that situation. One-page, clean, animated, mobile-ready. It's not a placeholder; it's a real site that can grow with you. And if the timing is genuinely off, I'm happy to stay in touch for when the next drop is coming.

---

**Objection 3: "We're happy with what we have."**

> Good — that's not a bad place to be. Out of curiosity, is the site converting the way you want it to, or is it more of a box you've checked? Most brands I talk to aren't unhappy with what they have, they're just not sure what better looks like. Happy to show you without any pressure to move forward.

---

**Objection 4: "How do I know it'll actually look like the demo?"**

> The demo is built with the same tools and process I use for client projects. The Nightshift Supply site is a real build — not a mock-up. The same animated components, the same code quality, the same deployment. The only thing that changes is your brand, your products, and your colors.

---

**Objection 5: "We have a developer on the team already."**

> That's great — I'm not replacing a dev, I'm a specialist. Most in-house devs are focused on functionality, not motion design or brand experience. What I bring is the aesthetic layer — the animations, the visual design, the drop page atmosphere. Happy to scope something that complements what your team already has.
