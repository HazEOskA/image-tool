## Lead Form Fields

Use these fields for an inquiry form or intake form. Can be implemented via Typeform, Tally, Notion form, or a custom form on the site.

---

### Form Title
**"Start Your NEONWERKS Launch Pack"**

### Form Subtitle
*Fill this out and we'll come back to you within 24 hours with next steps.*

---

### Fields

| Field Label | Type | Required | Notes |
|-------------|------|----------|-------|
| **Brand Name** | Short text | Yes | "What's your brand called?" |
| **Product Type** | Multiple choice | Yes | Apparel / Accessories / Footwear / Mixed / Other |
| **Launch Date** | Date or short text | Yes | "When are you targeting your launch or drop?" |
| **Style Direction** | Paragraph text | Yes | "Describe your aesthetic in a few words. Reference brands, vibes, or images." |
| **Budget Range** | Multiple choice | Yes | Under $1K / $1K–$2K / $2K–$5K / $5K+ / Not sure yet |
| **Contact Email** | Email | Yes | Primary contact for project communication |
| **Instagram Handle** | Short text | No | "@yourbrand — helps us understand your visual style" |
| **Anything else?** | Paragraph text | No | Open field for anything the client wants to add |
