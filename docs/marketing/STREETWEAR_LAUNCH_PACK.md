> A productized web offer for independent clothing brands, drop-based labels, and streetwear founders who need a site that moves as fast as their drops.

## Offer Name

**NEONWERKS Streetwear Launch Pack**
*"Your brand deserves a site built for the drop economy."*

---

## Target Customer

Independent streetwear brands and clothing labels that:

- Have product ready but no serious web presence
- Are doing drops via Instagram DMs, Linktree, or Shopify basics
- Want a site that looks as intentional as their pieces
- Have a launch window coming up (4–12 weeks out)
- Are not developers and don't want to manage a dev team

Specific personas:
- Solo founder, 500–10K followers, first or second collection
- Small team (2–4 people), doing $1K–$20K/month in revenue
- Creative director who already knows the vision but can't execute it digitally

---

## The Problem

Most streetwear brands launch with something that embarrasses the product.

A template Shopify. A link-in-bio page. A Carrd site from 2021. The clothes are clean — the web presence isn't. Customers notice. Stockists notice. Press notices.

The problem isn't effort. It's that building a custom site is expensive, slow, and usually means explaining your vision to someone who doesn't understand the culture.

---

## The Promise

**A motion-forward, conversion-ready web experience — built around your drop, delivered in weeks, not months.**

No generic templates. No dev handoff chaos. No "just pick a theme."

NEONWERKS builds sites that feel alive — fluid animations, neon-lit aesthetics, dark-mode-first, built for mobile scroll culture. Sites that hold attention long enough to convert it.

---

## What the Client Sends

The client provides:

- Brand name, tagline, and short brand story (can be rough)
- Product photos or lookbook (minimum 5–10 images)
- Colorway / palette preferences (or "match the pieces")
- Drop name, price points, and any product descriptions
- Social handles and any existing links
- Launch date or target window
- Any reference sites they like the feel of

Optional but helpful:
- Logo file (SVG or high-res PNG preferred)
- Font preferences
- Mood board or Instagram aesthetic direction

---

## What NEONWERKS Delivers

Depending on tier (see below), NEONWERKS delivers:

- Custom-designed, motion-forward landing page or full site
- Hero section with animated visuals and drop countdown (where applicable)
- Product or collection showcase section
- Brand story / About section
- CTA and contact / purchase flow
- Mobile-first, performance-optimized build
- Deployed to a live URL (Vercel or client's preferred host)
- Handoff documentation so the client can update text/images independently

## Revision Policy

- **Starter:** 1 round of revisions (up to 3 change requests per round)
- **Pro:** 2 rounds of revisions
- **Launch Max:** 3 rounds of revisions + post-launch support for 30 days

A revision is defined as a set of requested changes submitted in a single document or message. Additional rounds are billed at $120/round.

Scope changes after build begins (e.g., adding a new page, changing the core concept) are quoted separately.
